# Babbel'r Community Guidelines — draft for publication

Babbel'r is an adults-only language-practice community. You must be at least 18
to create an account. Do not create a profile for a minor or use Babbel'r to
contact, groom, exploit, or endanger a child.

## Be respectful

Use profiles and private chats to arrange consensual, respectful language
practice. Do not post or send:

- sexual or pornographic content, sexual solicitation, or non-consensual
  intimate content;
- harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech, or discriminatory abuse;
- spam, scams, impersonation, or unlawful content;
- content that exposes another person's private information without consent.

Do not use Babbel'r to arrange paid sexual services or exploit another person.

## Profile review and reporting

Profile photos and descriptions are reviewed by a moderator before they are
shown to other members. Updates to those fields return the profile to review.
Members can report a profile or an individual chat message and can block another
member. A block removes the member from discovery and ends access to that match's
chat.

Reports are reviewed by the Babbel'r moderation contact. We may remove content,
restrict or close accounts, and take other steps appropriate to the report and
applicable law. Do not use the report feature for knowingly false or abusive
reports.

## Child safety

Babbel'r is for adults only. Child sexual abuse or exploitation, including
grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or child sexual abuse material, is strictly
prohibited. We will take appropriate action on suspected child-safety
violations, including removing content and reporting to the competent
authorities where required by applicable law.
